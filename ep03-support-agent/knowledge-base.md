# Knowledge base — the only source the agent may answer from

[REFUNDS] Full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. Email
billing@ with your order number; processed in 3-5 business days.

[INVOICES] All invoices: Settings → Billing → Invoice history. Downloadable
as PDF. Invoices are also emailed on the 1st of each month.

[PASSWORD] Reset emails come from no-reply@ and can take up to 10 minutes.
If nothing arrives, the address may differ from the account email — use
"Find my account" on the login page.

[CANCEL] Settings → Billing → Cancel plan. Access continues until the end of
the paid period. No cancellation fees.

[API-LIMITS] Starter: 60 requests/min, 10k/day. Pro: 600/min, 100k/day.
Enterprise: custom.

[EXPORT] Settings → Data → Export all. Delivered as a ZIP (JSON + CSV) by
email within 24 hours. Available on every plan, including after cancellation
for 30 days.

[TEAM-BILLING] Added seats are prorated: you pay only for the remaining days
of the current cycle, then full price from the next cycle.

[PRIVACY] Data is stored in EU-west (Frankfurt). We are GDPR compliant; DPA
available at /legal/dpa. We never train models on customer data.

[SSO] SAML SSO is included in the Business plan and above. Setup guide:
Settings → Security → SSO. Okta, Azure AD, and Google Workspace supported.
