# Who we sell to (ICP) — and the rules

## Criteria (the agent must quote the tag, verbatim)

- [ICP-1] Company size between 20 and 500 people
- [ICP-2] Industry: SaaS, e-commerce, or professional services
- [ICP-3] Stated budget of at least $500/month
- [ICP-4] Timeline inside the next 3 months
- [ICP-5] Role: founder, ops leader, or revenue leader

## Instant disqualifiers

- [DQ-1] Students, job seekers, interns, hobbyists, or vendors selling to us
- [DQ-2] Under 5 people with no stated budget

## Verdict rules

- HOT: at least 2 ICP tags, one of which is [ICP-3] or [ICP-4]
- NURTURE: at least 1 ICP tag, no DQ tags
- DISQUALIFY: any DQ tag applies
- Anything else, any contradiction, any doubt: REVIEW — a human looks at it
